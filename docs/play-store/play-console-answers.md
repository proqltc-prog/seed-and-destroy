## App content (Policy → App content)

### Privacy policy
https://proqltc-prog.github.io/seed-and-destroy/privacy.html

### App access
**All functionality is available without special access.** The account is created anonymously on the device; no login or password.

### Ads
**Yes, my app contains ads** (AdMob rewarded videos).

### Advertising ID
**Yes**, the app uses the advertising ID (through the Google Mobile Ads SDK) for **Advertising or marketing** and **Analytics**.

### Content rating (IARC questionnaire)
- Category: **Game**
- Violence: **Yes, cartoon / fantasy violence**: cartoon characters with a sword, guns and missiles destroy cartoon obstacles and cells; no blood, no realistic violence, no violence against humans.
- Fear / horror: No
- Sexuality: **No sexual content or nudity.** The theme is a cartoon race of sperm cells to an egg (biology), with no sexual acts, nudity or innuendo in images or texts.
  *Owner decision:* if a question asks specifically about "sexual references or innuendo" in general, answering **"mild"** is the cautious choice (a slightly higher rating is better than a rejection).
- Language (swearing): No; usernames are filtered for offensive words
- Controlled substances (drugs, alcohol, tobacco): No (the "pill" obstacle is a cartoon contraceptive pill, not a drug reference)
- Gambling / simulated gambling: **No** (mystery boxes are free, cannot be bought with money, no casino mechanics)
- Users can interact or exchange content: **Yes, limited**: usernames are visible on leaderboards and to friends; no chat, no messages, no user-generated content besides the username
- Shares the user's location with others: No
- Digital purchases: **No** (credits are earned in the game, never sold for money)
- Expected result: around PEGI 7 / Everyone 10+ (cartoon violence).

### Target audience and content
- Target age groups: **13–15, 16–17, 18 and over** (not under 13; the game asks the age and disables online features under 13)
- Could the app unintentionally appeal to children? **No**, it targets teenagers and adults (store texts written for that audience)
- Not part of the Families programme.

### Data safety
Data is **encrypted in transit** (HTTPS). Users can **request deletion** of their account and data by e-mail (proqltc@gmail.com), as stated in the privacy policy. No data is sold.

| Data type (Play category) | Collected | Shared | Purpose | Optional? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Personal info → **User IDs** (anonymous account id, username, friend code) | Yes | Yes (the anonymous account id only, with Google AdMob, to verify watched videos) | App functionality, Account management, Fraud prevention | Required |
| App activity → **Other user-generated content** / **App interactions** (scores, finished runs, gates, play time, ads watched per day) | Yes | No | App functionality, Fraud prevention (anti-cheat) | Required |
| Device or other IDs → **Advertising ID** (by the Google Mobile Ads SDK) | Yes | Yes (Google) | Advertising, Analytics, Fraud prevention | Required in the app with ads |
| Location → **Approximate location** (IP address, by the Google Mobile Ads SDK) | Yes | Yes (Google) | Advertising, Fraud prevention | Required in the app with ads |
| App info and performance → **Diagnostics** (by the Google Mobile Ads SDK) | Yes | Yes (Google) | Analytics, Fraud prevention | Required in the app with ads |

Not collected: name, e-mail address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, files, health, financial info, messages.
Check Google's own guidance for the Mobile Ads SDK before submitting: https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure
